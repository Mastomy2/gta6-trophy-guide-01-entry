Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours, 1 playthrough

Built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then partner and stranger strands, then collectibles, then activities, then the 100% checklist. Full detail per trophy is in its guide below.

    Before you start

    • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off for the whole run (GTABase).
    • Free-roam stays open after the credits — everything except the Chapter 1 meditation can wait until after the story.
    • Scan as you go: WAiNK-scan new vehicles and study animals through the binoculars during the story — that progress counts toward 100% later.
